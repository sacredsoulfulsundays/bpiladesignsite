A note from Bea

I never set out to write a book. I set out to make beautiful homes. But after more than forty years of walking into people's lives through their front doors, I kept seeing the same thing. When a space is cared for with intention, the people in it soften. They rest. They reconnect.

That is what Design Enlightenment means to me. Your home can hold you, and you can hold yourself a little more gently inside it.

These books are my way of sharing that with anyone who wants it, whether or not you ever hire a designer.

About the author

Bea Pila is an interior designer, author, speaker and creator of Design Enlightenment. She founded B Pila Design, a Miami interior design and sacred space studio, and brings more than forty years of experience to her work. Every week she hosts Sacred Soulful Sundays, a livestream about living with more peace and purpose.
